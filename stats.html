How Fix Rate is calculated

The Fix Rate is the proportion of problems identified that have been addressed or solved to the satisfaction of the community and the monitor. Calculation: (number of problems solved / number of problems identified) × 100.

Notes & coverage

Figures are compiled from active monitoring initiatives using EduCheck and cover monitoring of school projects in partner programmes. Reports include structured evidence and monitor verification.
