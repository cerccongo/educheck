How Fix Rate is calculated

The Fix Rate is the proportion of problems identified that have been addressed or solved to the satisfaction of the community and monitor. It is expressed as a percentage: (no. of problems solved / no. of problems identified) × 100.

Notes

Figures are drawn from monitoring initiatives run using EduCheck. The platform is used within specific initiatives and figures may cover projects monitored within partner programmes.
