Tracking service delivery where it matters most

EduCheck is a citizen monitoring platform developed to strengthen accountability in public services. It combines a simple Android app, focused training for monitors, and an open web presence so evidence can drive action.

The challenge

Projects and services are often promised in the name of development but fail to deliver. Broken water systems, absent teachers, and shortages of medicine are just a few examples. Without clear evidence, communities lose leverage to demand fixes.

Our approach

We enable local monitors to gather reliable, verifiable reports — including photos and geolocation — which are shared publicly to create accountability. Training and stakeholder engagement are core: the app is a tool, the process is the programme.

What monitors record

  • Problems identified
  • Actions proposed & solutions
  • Community reviews (awareness, satisfaction, need)
  • Access to information (contracts, budgets)
Monitor workflow

A complete monitoring cycle

  1. Train monitors — equip them to identify and document reliably.
  2. Collect field evidence — photos, GPS, structured forms.
  3. Publish & engage — reports appear on the website and are shared with stakeholders.
  4. Track fixes — monitors verify when problems are resolved; Fix Rate is measured.
