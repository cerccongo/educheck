About EduCheck

The challenge

Desks that have been broken for months. Latrines no one can use. Textbooks that never arrived. A teacher who does not show up. Pupils are promised many things in the name of education, but too often those promises are not delivered as intended. Students, school leaders and communities need practical ways to surface problems and push for solutions.

Our approach

EduCheck gives students a practical way to make their schools work better through monitoring. Inspired by open consumer feedback in the commercial sector, our platform makes monitoring public and real-time so feedback cannot be easily ignored.

Not just an app

EduCheck is part of a broader approach: in-depth training for monitors, structured engagement with stakeholders (local authorities, contractors, NGOs), and an accountability process that uses app data to create pressure and enable fixes.

Use and availability

EduCheck is used as part of specific monitoring initiatives. Monitors receive training before deployment. If you want EduCheck for an initiative promoting accountability, please contact us through the Contact page.
