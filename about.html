About EduCheck

The challenge

A water supply that stopped working months ago. Teachers who do not show up for work. A dispensary that has run out of medicine. Citizens are promised many things in the name of development but too often the promises are not delivered as intended. Monitors, implementers and communities need practical ways to surface problems and push for solutions.

Our approach

EduCheck gives citizens a practical way to make projects and services local to them work better through monitoring. Inspired by open consumer feedback in the commercial sector, our platform makes monitoring public and real-time so feedback cannot be easily ignored.

Not just an app

EduCheck is part of a broader approach: in-depth training for monitors, structured engagement with stakeholders (local authorities, contractors, NGOs), and an accountability process that uses app data to create pressure and enable fixes.

Use and availability

EduCheck is used as part of specific monitoring initiatives. Monitors receive training before deployment. If you want EduCheck for an initiative promoting accountability, please contact us through the Contact page.
