What EduCheck does

📝 Report issues

Describe a problem with a photo and location: infrastructure, teacher attendance, curriculum, safety or accessibility.

📋 Track progress

Every issue moves openly through reported, acknowledged, fixed and verified.

🏫 School directory

Browse schools by region and type, in list or map view, with contacts and issue status.

📊 Analytics

Resolution rates, categories and regional patterns, with export for reports and policy.

🌍 Many languages

French, English, Lingala and Kikongo, with accessibility in mind and an offline queue for weak connections.

⭐ Relevance feedback

Rate course content, teaching methods and cultural relevance to inform curriculum discussions.

The loop:

Reported→Acknowledged→Fixed→Verified

Built for everyone involved

Monitors

The idea behind it

EduCheck is inspired by the Integrity Clubs in schools programme of the Centre de Recherche sur l'Anti-Corruption (CERC), where students collect frontline evidence and advocate with school officials. CERC reports for 2018–2022:

204secondary schools monitored
3,060students trained as monitors
33%integrity issues resolved through advocacy
150,441students benefited

Figures from cerc.cd. This site is an independent prototype with demo data.
