About EduCheck

EduCheck is a lightweight, student-centred monitoring tool that helps learners, parents and communities report and follow up on problems in schools. It was developed as a prototype to support the Integrity Clubs programme run by the Centre de Recherche sur l'Anti-Corruption (CERC) and to demonstrate how local evidence can feed into better school management and policy.

Purpose

Make school issues visible, improve transparency in responses, and give students an active role in improving their learning environment.

How it works

Users report an issue with a short description, category, optional photo and location. Each report moves through stages: Reported → Acknowledged → Fixed → Verified.

Privacy & data

Demo data on this site is anonymized. Real deployments should include careful consent, privacy safeguards, and local governance.
