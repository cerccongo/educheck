What is EduCheck?

EduCheck (formerly DevelopmentCheck) is a mobile app and supporting approach developed by Educheck S.A. Monitors use the app to record problems, solutions, and community feedback while in the field. Submitted reports appear on this website to create incentives for timely fixes and keep citizens' voices central to service delivery.

EduCheck is designed for low-tech contexts: Android-first, simple UI, can be configured in any language and used after brief in-depth training for local monitors.
