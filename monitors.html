Monitors — tools, training, and process

What monitors record

Monitors capture structured information that makes it easy for implementers and stakeholders to respond:

  • Problems (description, photo, GPS)
  • Actions & proposed fixes
  • Community reviews: awareness, satisfaction, perceived need
  • Access to information: contracts, budgets, timelines

Fix Rate

The Fix Rate is the proportion of identified problems that are resolved to the satisfaction of the community and the monitor. Formula: (problems solved / problems identified) × 100. We publish this metric transparently so partners and communities can track progress.

Training and engagement

Monitors receive focused training in evidence collection, ethical reporting, and stakeholder engagement. We work with local authorities and implementing partners to ensure reports result in constructive responses.

Monitor toolkit

  1. Mobile app with offline capability
  2. Structured forms & photo evidence
  3. Guidance for access-to-information requests
  4. Templates for escalation & follow-up
