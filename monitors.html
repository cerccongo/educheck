Monitors and monitoring process

What monitors record

  • Problems identified (what is broken or missing)
  • Actions taken or proposed to fix problems
  • Community reviews: awareness, satisfaction, perceived need
  • Access to information: availability of contracts, budgets and documentation

Fix Rate

The Fix Rate is the proportion of problems identified that have been addressed or solved to the satisfaction of the community and the monitor. It is calculated as: (number of problems solved / number of problems identified) × 100.

Training and stakeholder engagement

Monitors undergo focused training to ensure consistent identification of issues, to gather reliable evidence, and to engage constructively with stakeholders so solutions can be implemented.
